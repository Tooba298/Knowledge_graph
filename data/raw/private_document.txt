Faryal Gilani was born in Faisalabad in 1946. She studied at Marlow University and later at Northgate College of Science. She received the Northgate Prize in 1994. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Gilani". In 1981, Dr. Gilani joined Calder Institute of Physics. Faryal Gilani's doctoral advisor was Junaid Awan. He later moved to Calder Institute of Technology. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Pendleton University.
